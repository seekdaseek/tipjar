⚡ Tip Jar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What we collect

Tip Jar collects only the information you voluntarily provide when registering your tip jar: your name, handle, Solana wallet address, and category. This data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database to display your profile to other users.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect private keys, seed phrases, passwords, payment information, device identifiers, or browsing history. We never access your wallet beyond the public address you provide.

3. Blockchain data

Transactions made through Tip Jar are recorded on the Solana blockchain, which is public and immutable. We have no control over this data.

4. Firebase

Creator profile data is stored using Google Firebase. Firebase may collect anonymous usage analytics. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Third parties

Tip Jar integrates with third-party wallets (Phantom, MetaMask) via deep links. We do not share your data with these services beyond what is necessary to complete a transaction.

6. Data deletion

To remove your profile from Tip Jar, contact us at @ochinimus on X and we will delete your data within 7 days.

7. Children

Tip Jar is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

8. Contact

Questions? Reach us at @ochinimus on X.
